Data Deletion Instructions - Facebook Login

Important: If you have accessed our AI Colors application using Facebook Login and wish to remove this access or request data deletion, please follow the instructions below.

Our Data Handling Policy

Data Collection: Our application only collects minimal information necessary for login functionality, primarily your Facebook User ID and email address (if granted permission). We do not collect or store sensitive personal information beyond what is required for basic account authentication.

Data Usage: The information collected is used solely for:

  • User authentication and account management
  • Providing personalized color scheme recommendations
  • Saving your generated color schemes to your account

Data Retention: We retain user data only as long as necessary to provide our services or as required by law.

Method 1: Remove App Access via Facebook Settings

To revoke our application's access to your Facebook account, follow these steps:

  1. Log in to your Facebook account
  2. Go to Settings & Privacy → Settings
  3. Click on Apps and Websites in the left sidebar
  4. Find "AI Colors" in the list of authorized applications
  5. Click on the application entry
  6. Click the "Remove" button to revoke access
  7. Confirm the removal when prompted
Note: Removing app access via Facebook will prevent our application from accessing your Facebook data, but it does not automatically delete data that may already be stored in our system.

Method 2: Request Complete Data Deletion

If you want to completely delete all data associated with your account from our systems, please contact us directly.

Contact Information for Data Deletion Requests

Email: privacy@ai-colors.com

Subject Line: Data Deletion Request - Facebook Login

Required Information for Processing Your Request:

  • Your Facebook User ID (if known) or the email address associated with your account
  • Confirmation that you want to delete all data associated with your account
  • Any additional verification information we may request to confirm your identity

Data Deletion Process

When you request data deletion:

  1. Request Acknowledgment: We will acknowledge receipt of your deletion request within 72 hours
  2. Identity Verification: We may request additional information to verify your identity and ensure the security of the deletion process
  3. Data Removal: Upon verification, we will delete all personal data associated with your account within 30 days
  4. Confirmation: You will receive confirmation once the deletion process is complete
Legal Requirements: Please note that some data may be retained for longer periods if required by law, regulation, or legitimate business purposes (such as preventing fraud or resolving disputes). However, this data will be de-identified where possible and will not be used for any other purposes.

What Data Will Be Deleted

When you request complete data deletion, the following information will be permanently removed from our systems:

  • Your Facebook User ID and associated login credentials
  • Email address and profile information
  • All saved color schemes and preferences
  • Usage analytics and activity logs associated with your account
  • Any other personal data collected through your use of our application

Alternative Contact Methods

If you experience any issues with the data deletion process or have questions about our data handling practices, you may also contact us through:

  • Support Portal: Available through our main application interface
  • Privacy Officer: For specific privacy-related inquiries

Response Time

We are committed to processing data deletion requests promptly:

  • Initial Response: Within 72 hours of receiving your request
  • Complete Deletion: Within 30 days of identity verification
  • Confirmation: You will receive written confirmation once deletion is complete
